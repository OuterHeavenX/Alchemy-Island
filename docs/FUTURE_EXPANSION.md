# Future Expansion — Not Implemented

Only after the vertical slice is play-tested and approved, a future phase could explore a larger discovery graph, animals, villages, population growth, deeper farming and metallurgy, steam power, industry, electrical infrastructure, vehicles, computers, aviation, additional islands, oceans, and eventually space travel.

The next phase should begin with player telemetry and observed sticking points, not more recipes. Likely technical investments would include splitting the renderer into modules, formal content validation, save migrations, authored audio and art, accessibility options, and integration tests for the recipe graph.
